# Community Conduct

Participants must keep project spaces professional, respectful, and safe.

Expected behavior includes:

- Discuss technical evidence rather than attacking people.
- Respect privacy, attribution, licenses, and project data boundaries.
- Welcome good-faith questions and corrections.
- Give actionable review feedback.
- Disclose conflicts of interest and security concerns privately.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate exposure of private
information, copyright infringement, credential exposure, and attempts to
exfiltrate private project data are not tolerated.

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may restrict
participation when needed to protect people or the project. Security and
privacy reports should use the private process described in `SECURITY.md`.
