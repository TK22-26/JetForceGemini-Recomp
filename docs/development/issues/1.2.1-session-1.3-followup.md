## Remaining reports and evidence

- [ ] **Vela rescue cutscene: stalls and sections that suddenly look low resolution.** In the closed 1.2.0 route, within-shot update gaps reached 115.634 ms in shot 379, 51.853 ms in 344, and 134.401 ms in the first occurrence of 380. Neighboring shots were substantially better. Queue/presentation waits reached 68.693 ms and offscreen queue-slot waits 65.090 ms; measured framebuffer GPU-fence waits were at most 1.390 ms in that shot. These overlapping waits are not additive CPU percentages.
- [ ] **Vela selection: slow/choppy and visibly lower detail in the hotfix preview.** The owner supplied a 1.2.1-beta.1 screenshot showing OUTPUT 27.9, SCENES 25.9, mean 35.9 ms and MAX 76.3 ms. This is reported visual/counter evidence, not a complete new timing qualification.
- [ ] **Boss combat: lower apparent resolution and output rate despite working controls.** The owner reported blur and slow presentation on entering Vela's boss fight. A preserved approximately 20-second 1.2.0 window has 599 game updates with every interval two VI, but only 45.869 output FPS. Native blur adds a second screen-sized pass at the same VI; the old scene counter therefore reports about 59.824 scene passes/sec. All 919 displayed outputs in the observed renderer window have source rate zero and sequence length one. Correcting that counter in 1.2.1 does not correct presentation.
- [ ] **Native motion blur/compositing loses high-resolution detail and interpolation.** Captured display lists identify a 623-command native blur pass sampling a 320x240 RGBA16 framebuffer. GPU framebuffer copies are disabled globally for CPU-written/softened shadow compatibility, so this effect samples the RAM image. The extra pass also replaces same-tick interpolation history. Preserve the intended blur, fades, HUD/reticles, CPU-written masks and submission ordering when fixing it. Do not remove the effect or globally enable framebuffer copies without validation.
- [ ] **Audio interruptions over the full long route.** Final play-health records 10 underruns and zero overruns. The rescue interval itself had zero underruns; detailed actor tracing reached its 256 MiB cap at approximately 949 seconds, so the ten events cannot all be assigned to particular scenes from that stream. Their existence is confirmed in the earlier long capture; persistence in stable 1.2.1 needs a clean comparison.
- [ ] **Severe invasion-replay slowdown reported in #19.** The reporter sees 1-2 FPS with Unlimited + VSync on an RTX 5090. The owner's SSD capture on a 5090 looked fine. Keep #19 open; this difference does not establish an I/O cause or a fix.
- [ ] **Continue the existing general 1.3 performance qualification.** Finish the opening-village/cutscene scheduling work and combat/update pacing work, including the rare missed updates at high output targets. Retain the prior Goldwood ship-cutscene and aspect-ratio candidates and their owner observations, then qualify the combined changes. A stable output counter does not establish smooth game updates.
