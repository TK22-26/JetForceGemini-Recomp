## Symptoms and reproduction

What the user observes, expected behavior, minimal steps, frequency and location.

## Evidence and hypotheses

Identify capture/build/input/save provenance (hashes or artifact IDs). Separate
measured observations from suspected causes. Note confounders and invalid evidence.

## Attempts and results

| Date | Hypothesis and change/experiment | Result | Decision and evidence |
|---|---|---|---|

Retain failures and rejected approaches that inform the next decision.

## Actual resolution

Pending until a cause-level fix is verified. Then describe the demonstrated cause,
the final change and why it addresses it. Link code and actual commit/release if
available. State whether it is in source, a private candidate, installed preview,
or released. Do not call a mitigation a complete resolution.

## Verification and remaining limits

Record exact checks and relevant measurements, behavior/speed/save compatibility,
regression coverage and live verification. State what was not tested and the
remaining acceptance criteria. Add reopen criteria where useful.

## Lessons for future ports and changes

Document transferable constraints and implications (including timing, rendering,
input and future higher-refresh support where relevant), with primary sources.
